About

The Argentina Economic Monitor is a project of Economic Order, by Nicolás Cachanosky. Data are pulled automatically from official sources each day, validated, and stored with their full revision history.

Source data: INDEC and other official agencies via datos.gob.ar (CC BY 4.0). Charts and transformations are ours; errors are ours too. Corrections are welcome on GitHub.

Data

Each chart offers the series as CSV. Full revision histories (every published vintage) are in data/vintages/.
